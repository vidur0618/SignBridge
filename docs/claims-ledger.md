## Approved public summary today

> SignBridge Reception is a locally implemented hybrid prototype. Its production-target lane is a bounded catalog of human-recorded phrases that remains disabled pending independent Deaf review and rights. Its opt-in Hand Talk WebGL lane is experimental; no token or provider output has yet been tested, and it must not be described as unrestricted accurate or certified interpretation. English captions remain the primary fallback.

Claims must always distinguish implemented behavior, local verification, production observation, estimate, target, vendor assertion, and independent human acceptance.

Primary policy and provider references: [Hand Talk quick start](https://api-docs.handtalk.me/v1/en/javascript/getting-start), [fixed release channels](https://api-docs.handtalk.me/v1/en/javascript/release-channels), [SDK limitations](https://api-docs.handtalk.me/beta/en/introduction), and the [WFD/WASLI avatar statement](https://wfdeaf.org/wp-content/uploads/WFD-and-WASLI-Statement-on-Avatar-FINAL-14032018-Updated-14042018.pdf).
