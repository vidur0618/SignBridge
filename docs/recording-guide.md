# ASL phrase recording guide

This guide is subordinate to the signer and reviewer. It does not specify ASL wording.

## Session preparation

- Lock the bounded reception intent and pilot-site truth conditions before recording.
- Confirm compensation, consent, commercial use, contest use, hosting, attribution, withdrawal, and publicity choices in writing.
- Record the slate and consent records privately; do not place identities in filenames or Git.
- Use a stable camera, diffuse front lighting, uncluttered high-contrast background, and clothing that preserves hand visibility.

## Frame and export

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, landscape, camera near eye level.
- Keep face, hands, torso, and the full intended signing space in frame from lead-in through lead-out.
- Record complete utterances at natural speed with natural facial and body grammar.
- Export one muted H.264 MP4 per approved utterance.
- Do not mirror, crop, time-stretch, splice isolated signs, remove required non-manuals, or use generative fill.

## Review checklist

The independent Deaf reviewer evaluates meaning preservation, omissions/additions, grammar, regional fit, facial/non-manual signals, timing, framing, orientation, caption pairing, and comprehensibility in the actual interface. Approval must name the exact SHA-256 and catalog version.
