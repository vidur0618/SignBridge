# Paid pilot runbook

## Before opening

1. Record the deployed commit, Cloud Run revision, catalog version, and environment mode.
2. Verify that the catalog is published, all assets return matching hashes, and no asset is withdrawn.
3. Test microphone denial, live captions, one supported intent, one unsupported case, typing, manual phrase selection, replay, pause, and session clearing.
4. Confirm the site's actual human escalation path and remove any phrase that promises unavailable support.
5. Train staff: captions are provisional until finalized; review every intent; reject anything wrong; never use the product for excluded communication.

## During the pilot

- Obtain visible, informed participation consent before processing audio.
- Keep a staff member present and allow the Deaf visitor to choose captions-only or typing.
- Do not record names, contact information, transcripts, or private comments in product telemetry.
- Stop and use the site's normal accommodation process for high-stakes or complex communication.
- Report an unclear or incorrect phrase by structured session ID and asset ID; do not copy the conversation into the feedback form.

## Commercial and contest evidence

Keep the invoice, payment receipt, simple P&L, customer agreement, consented testimonial, user/customer counts, aggregate usage dashboard, Cloud/Gemini invocation evidence, deployment record, and actual expenses in a private evidence system. Related-party revenue must be separated.
